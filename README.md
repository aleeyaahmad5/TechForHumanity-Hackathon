## Assumptions Baked Into This Version

- **2-day format** (Sat–Sun) recommended over 3-day, given intern organizing capacity — see [02_Executive_Summary.md](02_Executive_Summary.md).
- **Total capacity ceiling: 150** — everyone combined (participants + mentors + judges + volunteers + organizers, ~10–20 of that reserved for staff). Participant registration target: 50–130, more the better.
- **Deliberately low-budget model:** no ticket over $35, venue/AV/volunteer costs at $0 (RMIT covers venue and AV), catering scales with headcount up to ~$1,200 at the 150-person ceiling via [Catering At Its Finest](https://www.cateringatitsfinest.com/) grazing boxes, one meal (lunch) a day only. **Ticket sales alone cover this across the full 50–150 total-head range — no sponsor cash is required.** See [05_Budget_Revenue.md](05_Budget_Revenue.md).
- **TI MedTech is confirmed at $0 cash sponsorship.** It's a logo + in-kind partner only. Sponsorship of any kind (from any partner) is pure upside (better prizes, extra food), not a budget requirement — see [01](01_TI_MedTech_Partnership_Clarification.md).
- **The event is Melbourne-only.** There is no Singapore leg, venue, or partner track.
- **TI MedTech's confirmed contribution:** Tita (CEO) and/or Dr Irwan (CTO) judge, joining virtually, and separately host a live virtual workshop on Day 1 (~4:00–4:45pm) — see [09_Participant_Experience.md](09_Participant_Experience.md) and [14_OnDay_Playbook_Melbourne.md](14_OnDay_Playbook_Melbourne.md). This is the full extent of TI MedTech's commitment — no further ask.
- **Pre-event mixer: Friday 20 Nov, 6:00–8:00pm, free, RMIT.** Opt-in only, for people still looking for a team or wanting to casually network — not part of core registration.
- **Judging is weighted toward innovation, not technical execution** — a working codebase is not required; a pitch deck, prototype, or mockup is judged on equal footing. See [09_Participant_Experience.md](09_Participant_Experience.md) and [TEMPLATES/Judging_Rubric.md](TEMPLATES/Judging_Rubric.md).
- **Leon's role is deliberately limited:** planning input and occasional sponsor introductions only. He is not the RMIT venue contact (that's Prof. Michael A Cowling, direct) and does not lead sponsor or mentor/judge outreach (that's Kashaf & Aleeya) — see [06_Sponsor_Plan.md](06_Sponsor_Plan.md).
