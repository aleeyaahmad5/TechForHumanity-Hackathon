# 09 — Participant Journey Map

Adapted to the recommended 2-day format (Sat 21 – Sun 22 Nov 2026). See [13_Participant_Storyboard](13_Participant_Storyboard.md) for the narrative version of this same journey.

## 1. Awareness (Weeks 3–6)
- **Touchpoint:** Instagram, LinkedIn, or uni club poster.
- **Thought:** "Is this for me? Can I really build something?"
- **Emotion:** Curious but uncertain.
- **Opportunity:** Remove barriers — emphasize "all skill levels welcome" in every asset (see [10_Marketing_Recruitment](10_Marketing_Recruitment.md)).

## 2. Registration (Weeks 3–15)
- **Touchpoint:** 5-field registration form.
- **Thought:** "How do I register? What does the form ask?"
- **Emotion:** Excited but possibly overwhelmed by a long form.
- **Opportunity:** Keep it to 5 core fields + accessibility/dietary; instant confirmation email with calendar invite.

## 3. Pre-Event (Week 15)
- **Touchpoint:** Mentorship schedule, team assignment info.
- **Thought:** "What's the vibe? Do I know anyone? Who should I follow?"
- **Emotion:** Anticipation, slight anxiety about teamwork.
- **Opportunity:** Introduction email, team chat link, mentor bios, optional pre-event Discord call.

## 4. Opening (Day 1, 9:00–10:00am)
- **Touchpoint:** Arrival, registration table, name tag, swag.
- **Thought:** "Where do I sit? Who's speaking? Is this real?"
- **Emotion:** Nervous energy, excited.
- **Opportunity:** Warm welcome, clear seating, icebreaker, inspiring opening from Leon or a TI MedTech leader.

## 5. Hacking (Day 1, 10:00am–9:00pm)
- **Touchpoint:** Team workspace, mentor office hours.
- **Thought:** "Are we building something good? Should we pivot?"
- **Emotion:** Flow state (ideally), occasional frustration, camaraderie.
- **Opportunity:** Structured office hours, a visible progress/sharing wall, steady snack supply.

## 6. Demo Day (Day 2, 2:15–4:00pm)
- **Touchpoint:** Pitch to judges, watch other teams present.
- **Thought:** "Did we win? What did others build?"
- **Emotion:** Pride, adrenaline, inspiration from peers.
- **Opportunity:** Every team presents (not just finalists); judges give constructive feedback; highlight the diversity of solutions.

## 7. Closing (Day 2, 4:00–5:30pm)
- **Touchpoint:** Awards ceremony, networking.
- **Thought:** "I did it. Who do I stay connected with?"
- **Emotion:** Accomplishment, gratitude, energy depletion.
- **Opportunity:** Multiple award categories beyond 1st place; structured LinkedIn/contact exchange; announce a follow-up touchpoint.

## 8. Post-Event (Weeks 1–4 after)
- **Touchpoint:** Survey, mentor intros, sponsor follow-up.
- **Thought:** "What's next? Can I keep building?"
- **Emotion:** Reflection, gratitude.
- **Opportunity:** Email with participant/mentor connections, sponsor job postings, optional follow-up webinar — see [12_Evaluation_PostEvent](12_Evaluation_PostEvent.md).
