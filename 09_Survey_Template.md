# 09 — Post-Event Participant Survey Template

Send within 24 hours of event close (see [03_Project_Plan_WBS](03_Project_Plan_WBS.md) task 10.1). Maps directly to the HEART targets in [02_Executive_Summary](02_Executive_Summary.md).

| # | Question | Type | HEART dimension |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | On a scale of 0–10, how likely are you to recommend this hackathon to a friend? | NPS (0–10) | Happiness |
| 2 | Overall, how satisfied were you with the event? | 1–5 scale | Happiness |
| 3 | Rate: mentorship quality | 1–5 scale | Engagement |
| 4 | Rate: team experience | 1–5 scale | Engagement |
| 5 | Rate: venue & logistics | 1–5 scale | Engagement |
| 6 | Rate: catering | 1–5 scale | Engagement |
| 7 | Rate: judges & judging process | 1–5 scale | Engagement |
| 8 | Did you learn a new skill this weekend? | Yes/No + open text | Adoption |
| 9 | Did you make a connection (mentor or peer) you plan to keep in touch with? | Yes/No | Adoption |
| 10 | Would you attend a future TI MedTech / RMIT event? | Yes/No/Maybe | Retention |
| 11 | Would you want to join a mentorship program if one were offered? | Yes/No | Retention |
| 12 | What was your favorite moment? | Open text | Happiness (qualitative) |
| 13 | What would you change? | Open text | — (product feedback) |
| 14 | (Optional, anonymous) Demographic info for diversity reporting | Multi-select | — (sponsor/inclusion reporting) |

**Distribution:** email link + QR code on the closing slide. **Owner:** Aleeya. **Target response rate:** ≥ 50% of attendees (chase with one reminder at 48 hours).
