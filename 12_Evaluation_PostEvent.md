# 12 — Evaluation & Post-Event Plan

## Participant Survey

Sent within 24 hours of event close. Full template: [09_Survey_Template.md](09_Survey_Template.md).

## Metrics to Track

| Category | Metric |
|---|---|
| Attendance | # registered vs. # attended (conversion rate) |
| Engagement | # teams formed, average team size, mentor sessions attended per team |
| Submission quality | # teams submitted, judge scores, solution viability |
| Sponsor ROI | mentor/judge hours delivered, leads generated, social reach |
| Inclusivity | # under-represented participants, # accessibility accommodations used |
| Post-hackathon | # participants connecting with mentors on LinkedIn, # continuing to build, # interested in future events |

These roll up directly into the HEART targets set in [02_Executive_Summary](02_Executive_Summary.md) — don't track new metrics that aren't already implied there.

## Sponsor Handoff (Week 16, within 1 week post-event)

Deliver to each sponsor (TI MedTech gets a short thank-you + summary, consistent with their confirmed judge + workshop scope — see [01](01_TI_MedTech_Partnership_Clarification.md)):

- Deck with event photos, participant demographics, judge feedback, and sponsor-specific ROI (mentors engaged, leads, brand reach)
- Participant list for future outreach, **only where consent was captured at registration**
- A short thank-you note

## Lessons Learned Session (Week 17)

- Team debrief: what went well, what didn't, what changes next time
- Document in the shared Drive (see Archival below) — this becomes the seed for next year's plan

## Financial Close-out

- Final expense reconciliation against [05_Budget_Revenue](05_Budget_Revenue.md)
- Calculate actual profit to Kashaf & Aleeya (per the revenue model)
- Document for TI MedTech finance / accounting records

## Archival & Documentation

- Save photos, feedback, survey results to a shared Google Drive
- Write a 1-page summary for whoever organizes next year
- Thank volunteers and mentors individually — a short personal note goes further than a mass email, and keeps them warm for next year's mentor pool ([07_Volunteer_Staffing](07_Volunteer_Staffing.md), [06_Sponsor_Plan](06_Sponsor_Plan.md))
