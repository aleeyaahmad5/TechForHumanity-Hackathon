# Judging Rubric — Tech for Humanity

Distribute to judges at the pre-demo briefing (1:00–1:30pm Day 2, see [14_OnDay_Playbook_Melbourne](../14_OnDay_Playbook_Melbourne.md)). Score each team 1–10 per criterion; weighted total determines placement.

| Criterion | Weight | 1–3 (Weak) | 4–6 (Solid) | 7–10 (Excellent) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Innovation** | 25% | Derivative of existing tools, no clear angle | A reasonable twist on a known approach | A genuinely novel approach to the problem |
| **Impact** | 25% | Vague or unmeasurable benefit | Plausible benefit to a defined user group | Clear, scalable, measurable benefit to under-served communities |
| **Execution** | 20% | Doesn't run / barely functional | Core flow works, rough edges | Polished, working demo, handles edge cases |
| **Presentation** | 15% | Hard to follow, no clear ask | Clear structure, some engagement | Compelling story, confident delivery, clear next step |
| **Social Responsibility** | 15% | No consideration of accessibility/ethics/inclusion | Some consideration | Accessibility, ethics, and inclusivity are core to the design, not an afterthought |

**Bonus (judge discretion, does not add to the 100% total but breaks ties):** the solution includes concrete accessibility features, or centers the voice/needs of a marginalized group directly in its design.

**Scoring sheet columns:** Team Name | Innovation (/10) | Impact (/10) | Execution (/10) | Presentation (/10) | Social Responsibility (/10) | Weighted Total | Judge Notes

**Award categories** (see [09_Participant_Experience](../09_Participant_Experience.md)): 1st, 2nd, 3rd, People's Choice (audience vote, tallied separately), Best Social Impact, Best Presentation.
