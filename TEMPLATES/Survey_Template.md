# Generic Post-Event Survey Template

Reusable structure for any future Tech for Humanity event (this year's filled-in version with HEART mapping: [09_Survey_Template.md](../09_Survey_Template.md)).

1. NPS: "How likely are you to recommend this event to a friend?" (0–10)
2. Overall satisfaction (1–5)
3. Satisfaction by area: mentorship, team experience, venue/logistics, catering, judging (1–5 each)
4. Skill/learning gained (Yes/No + open text)
5. New connection made and intend to keep (Yes/No)
6. Interest in future events (Yes/No/Maybe)
7. Interest in a mentorship program, if offered (Yes/No)
8. Favorite moment (open text)
9. What would you change (open text)
10. Optional anonymous demographic questions, for diversity/inclusion reporting only — never required

**Distribution rule:** send within 24 hours of event close, one reminder at 48 hours, close the survey after 1 week so results can feed the sponsor report deadline.
