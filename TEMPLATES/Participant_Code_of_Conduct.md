# Participant Code of Conduct — Tech for Humanity

Acknowledged at registration (checkbox, [09_Participant_Experience](../09_Participant_Experience.md)) and reiterated in the opening welcome ([14_OnDay_Playbook_Melbourne](../14_OnDay_Playbook_Melbourne.md), 9:00–9:15am).

## Expected Behavior

- Treat every participant, mentor, judge, sponsor, and volunteer with respect, regardless of background, skill level, identity, or the "polish" of their idea.
- Give and receive feedback constructively — this event exists to help people learn, not to gatekeep.
- Respect the space: keep noise reasonable in quiet zones, clean up after yourself, follow venue safety instructions.
- Respect consent — for photos/video (opt-out available), for personal space, for anyone who steps away from a conversation.

## Not Tolerated

- Harassment of any kind (verbal, physical, or via any communication channel used during the event, including Discord/Slack)
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Sabotage of another team's work
- Sharing another participant's personal information without consent

## Reporting

If something happens, tell any volunteer or go directly to the Participant Experience Lead — see the escalation path in [14_OnDay_Playbook_Melbourne](../14_OnDay_Playbook_Melbourne.md). Reports are handled confidentially. Organizers (Kashaf & Aleeya) reserve the right to remove anyone from the event for violating this code, without refund.

## Consent

By registering, participants consent to:
- This code of conduct
- Photo/video use for event promotion (opt-out available — flag at registration)
- Contact from event sponsors post-event, **only if** they opted in separately (see [12_Evaluation_PostEvent](../12_Evaluation_PostEvent.md) sponsor handoff)
