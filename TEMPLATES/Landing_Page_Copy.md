## What

A weekend hackathon where student teams build solutions to real problems facing under-served communities — health, education, housing, and accessibility. Mentored by industry professionals, judged by people who've actually shipped products.

## When

Saturday 28 – Sunday 29 November 2026. Full run-of-show shared closer to the date.

## Where

Melbourne: RMIT Activator Hub. Singapore: venue to be announced. Simultaneous events, same weekend.

## Why

Students with talent and ideas often don't get a low-barrier way to test them against real industry feedback. This is that chance — with mentorship, a real judging panel, and prizes that go beyond cash (see below).

## Who

All skill levels welcome — coders, designers, writers, and first-time hackers alike. Teams are matched for a mix of skills, so you don't need to arrive with a team or a specific technical background.

## Prizes

1st, 2nd, 3rd place, People's Choice, Best Social Impact, Best Presentation — plus every team leaves with a shareable certificate and, for winning teams, an introduction to TI MedTech.
