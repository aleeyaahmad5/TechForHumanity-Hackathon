## Cost Optimization

- **In-kind over cash:** every mentor, judge, or prize sourced through Leon's network is a direct expense-line reduction, not just a nice-to-have — prioritize in-kind asks in sponsor conversations (see [06_Sponsor_Plan](06_Sponsor_Plan.md)).
- **University club volunteer labor:** removes any staffing cost line entirely; budget only appreciation (food/swag), not honorariums.
- **Digital-first marketing:** near-zero cost given social + university mailing lists; the $200 line is buffer, not baseline spend.
- **TI MedTech catering/logistics support:** if Scenario B ([01](01_TI_MedTech_Partnership_Clarification.md)) materializes, ask specifically for catering or venue cost coverage — the two largest line items.

## Breakeven Analysis (Melbourne)

- Fixed costs not covered by sponsorship cash: $9,713 − $8,500 = **$1,213**
- At $28 blended ticket price: **breakeven ≈ 44 paid tickets**
- Target range is 50–80 registrations (63 paid assumed) → breakeven is achievable within the low end of the target, meaning **profit is realistic even if registration undershoots target**, provided the $8,500 sponsorship cash figure holds.
- **Sensitivity:** if TI MedTech lands at only $2,000 (below even Scenario A) or a Gold/Silver sponsor falls through, breakeven rises to ~75 tickets — near the top of the target range. Treat sponsorship confirmation (M4, Week 3) as the single biggest lever on profit, more than ticket sales volume.
