# 07 — Volunteer & Staffing Plan

Melbourne-focused; Singapore mirrors this structure at a smaller scale once the local partner is confirmed ([01](01_TI_MedTech_Partnership_Clarification.md) Q4).

## Key Roles

| Role | Responsibility | Headcount (MEL) |
|---|---|---|
| Venue Lead | Room setup, AV, catering liaison | 1–2 |
| Tech Lead | WiFi, power, registration tech, judging platform | 2 |
| Mentorship Coordinator | Office-hours scheduling, mentor matching | 1–2 |
| Judge Coordinator | Judge intros, scoring collation, demo day logistics | 1 |
| Participant Experience Lead | Registration, team formation, comms | 2 |
| Logistics Lead | Catering, supplies, contingency response | 2 |
| Social Media / Comms | Live updates, photo/video coverage | 1–2 |
| General floaters (buffer) | Cover no-shows, overflow tasks | 3–4 |

**Total estimate: 15–20 volunteers for Melbourne**, recruited with a ~25% buffer against no-shows (see [11_Risk_Register](11_Risk_Register.md)). Singapore headcount TBD, likely scaled to venue size once confirmed.

## Recruitment Channels

- University clubs (RMIT Activator, Melbourne Uni, Monash, Swinburne)
- TI MedTech team (Dr Irwan, Tita, other interns)
- Leon's network — reserved for senior/specialist roles only (Tech Lead, Judge Coordinator) if student volunteers can't cover them
- Open call on social media & email (see [10_Marketing_Recruitment](10_Marketing_Recruitment.md))

## Training Checklist

- [ ] Code of conduct & safety briefing (see [TEMPLATES/Participant_Code_of_Conduct](TEMPLATES/Participant_Code_of_Conduct.md) — volunteers hold participants to the same standard)
- [ ] Role-specific responsibilities & escalation path (who do I tell, for what — see [14_OnDay_Playbook_Melbourne](14_OnDay_Playbook_Melbourne.md) escalation table)
- [ ] Participant accommodation needs (accessibility, dietary, tech support — sourced from registration data)
- [ ] Emergency procedures (first aid location, fire exits, assembly point)
- [ ] Communication protocols (shared Slack/WhatsApp channel, check-in cadence)

## Shift Schedule (2-day format)

| Shift | Day 1 (Sat) | Day 2 (Sun) |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | 8:00am–2:00pm | 8:00am–2:00pm |
| Afternoon/Evening | 1:30pm–9:30pm | 1:30pm–6:00pm |

Every role should have at least 2 people covering it across shifts so no single volunteer works a full 13-hour day. Cross-train Venue Lead ↔ Logistics Lead and Participant Experience ↔ Judge Coordinator so shifts are interchangeable if someone no-shows (see [11_Risk_Register](11_Risk_Register.md) — Volunteer no-show risk).
