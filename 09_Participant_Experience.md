# 09 — Participant Experience (UX-Informed)

## Problem Statement (Participant Perspective)

> Students in under-served communities — and students who want to build for them — want to apply their skills to solve real problems, gain industry mentorship, and build a professional network, but lack access to these opportunities.

Full journey map: [09_Journey_Map.md](09_Journey_Map.md).

## Team Formation Mechanics

| Option | Description | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| A — Random | Encourages diverse skill mix, reduces cliques | Risk of unbalanced skill sets |
| B — Skill-based | Participants self-report skill level at registration; matched for complementary skills | Best balance of diversity + capability — **recommended** |
| C — Open | First-come-first-served, friends can team up | Risk of skill imbalance and pre-existing cliques dominating |

**Recommendation: Option B.** It directly serves the "all skill levels welcome" positioning in marketing ([10_Marketing_Recruitment](10_Marketing_Recruitment.md)) without leaving a technical team without any designer, or vice versa.

## Mentorship Model

- **Office hours (2-day format):** Saturday 1:00–3:00pm & 5:00–6:00pm; Sunday 10:00am–12:00pm (final session before submission deadline). Each slot: 15-minute rotating check-ins per team.
- **Mentor brief:** sent 1 week before event (Week 15) with team introductions and suggested questions to probe (see [03_Project_Plan_WBS](03_Project_Plan_WBS.md) task 7.3).
- **Mentor network:** Leon's connections (enterprise), TI MedTech, RMIT Activator, university faculty.
- **Estimated:** 3–5 mentors for Melbourne, rotated across the three office-hours slots so no team goes a full day without input.

## Judging Criteria & Rubric

Framed around "Tech for Humanity":

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Innovation — novel approach, creative thinking | 25% |
| Impact — addresses a real problem, scalable, measurable benefit | 25% |
| Execution — code quality, polish, demo clarity | 20% |
| Presentation — clear pitch, engaging story | 15% |
| Social Responsibility — accessibility, ethics, inclusivity | 15% |
| Bonus | Extra judge discretion if the solution includes accessibility features or centers marginalized voices |

## Accessibility Accommodations

**Registration form captures:**
- Dietary restrictions (vegan, halal, gluten-free, allergies, nut-free)
- Mobility needs (wheelchair access, standing desk)
- Sensory needs (quiet space, lighting sensitivity, ASL interpreter)
- Neurodivergence accommodations (breaks, fidget tools, reduced stimulation)
- Tech access (shared laptop needed? screen reader support?)
- Childcare needs (inquire early; not guaranteed)

**On-the-day setup:** quiet room, accessible bathroom signage, fidget tools on tables, flexible break schedule, live captions for speeches where feasible, accessible parking info. Full checklist: [08_Venue_Logistics](08_Venue_Logistics.md).
