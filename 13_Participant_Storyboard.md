## Narrative Summary

Alex's journey moves from discovery, to belonging, to challenge, to pride, to continued growth. The event removes barriers — an accessible ticket price, "all levels welcome" messaging, a skill-matched team, supportive mentors — and produces meaningful outcomes: a social-impact award, mentor relationships, and continued building after the weekend ends. This is the experience every logistics and program decision in this plan should be checked against.

Participant testimonials (once collected post-event) should replace this illustrative persona in future marketing — see [10_Marketing_Recruitment](10_Marketing_Recruitment.md) Email 2.
