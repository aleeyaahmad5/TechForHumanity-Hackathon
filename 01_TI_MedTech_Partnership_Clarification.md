## Why This Is the Right Outcome

- Kashaf & Aleeya are interns with a fixed weekly time budget, leading sponsor outreach and mentor/judge sourcing themselves (see [06_Sponsor_Plan](06_Sponsor_Plan.md)) — a deeper co-producer relationship would have spread them too thin.
- The event is now Melbourne-only (see below), which removes the one thing that would have justified a larger TI MedTech ask (a Singapore venue/partner introduction).
- A broader "under-served communities" theme, kept unnarrowed, is more defensible for cross-industry sponsor outreach than a MedTech-specific one.
- The budget was never built to depend on TI MedTech funding, so this outcome changes nothing about financial feasibility — see [05_Budget_Revenue](05_Budget_Revenue.md).
