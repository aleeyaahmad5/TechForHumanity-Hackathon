## 5 Clarifying Questions for TI MedTech

Ask these in order on the Week 1 call. Record answers verbatim; they become the source of truth for Deliverables 05 and 06.

1. **Brand relationship:** Does TI MedTech want to be listed as the **primary sponsor** (logo + funding, hands-off) or **co-producer** (shared billing, active role in programming and judging)?
2. **People commitment:** Will TI MedTech provide **judges, mentors, or speakers** — and if so, how many people, for how many hours, on which day(s)?
3. **Budget commitment:** Is there a **dollar allocation** from TI MedTech for prizes, catering, venue, or marketing — and is it a fixed amount or matched/conditional?
4. **Singapore ownership:** Does TI MedTech have an **existing venue, partner organization, or local contact in Singapore**, or does that need to be sourced independently (via Leon or cold outreach)? Who owns that relationship?
5. **Content alignment:** Does TI MedTech want the "Tech for Humanity" theme **narrowed toward MedTech/health-specific problem statements**, or kept broad across health, education, housing, and accessibility?

**2 additional critical questions** (add if time allows):

6. **IP & follow-on:** If a team builds something adjacent to TI MedTech's business, does TI MedTech want **right of first conversation** for venture support / internship pipelines, or no claim at all?
7. **Reporting expectations:** What does TI MedTech need **post-event** (ROI deck, participant list, photos, metrics) to justify this internally — and to whom does that report go?

## Scenario B — Full Scope (Co-Production)

| Commitment | Detail |
|---|---|
| Funding | Larger allocation, likely covering a defined budget line (e.g., all of Melbourne catering + prizes) rather than a flat sponsor fee |
| Branding | Co-billed as presenting partner ("Tech for Humanity, presented by TI MedTech") |
| People | 2–3 judges, 2–4 mentors across both cities, 1 speaking slot (keynote or panel) for Dr Irwan/Tita |
| Content | Theme narrows toward MedTech-adjacent problem statements; TI MedTech reviews judging rubric weighting |
| Singapore | TI MedTech identifies/introduces the Singapore venue and local partner |
| Reporting | Formal post-event ROI deck + participant list handoff (see [12_Evaluation_PostEvent](12_Evaluation_PostEvent.md)) |
| Activation time | **4–6 weeks** — needs a coordination meeting, content review cycles, and mentor/judge onboarding logistics on top of the sponsor agreement |

**What this unlocks:** Real de-risking of the Singapore track (venue + local partner sourced by someone with actual Singapore presence) and a stronger judging bench — but it adds a second stakeholder relationship (TI MedTech leadership) that Kashaf & Aleeya must manage in parallel with Leon, on top of their internship workload.

## Recommendation

**Start from Scenario A, and negotiate up only where TI MedTech volunteers more.** Reasoning:

- Kashaf & Aleeya are interns with a fixed weekly time budget; Leon is already covering enterprise sponsor outreach and mentor/judge sourcing (see [06_Sponsor_Plan](06_Sponsor_Plan.md)) — a second co-producer relationship to manage in parallel risks spreading both organizers too thin in the first 4 weeks, when venue and Singapore decisions are most time-critical.
- The one piece of Scenario B that materially changes feasibility — **a Singapore venue/partner lead** — should be asked for explicitly (Question 4) regardless of which scenario TI MedTech prefers. If TI MedTech can make an introduction without taking on full co-production, that's the highest-leverage outcome of the call.
- Scenario B's content-alignment clause (narrowing the theme) is worth resisting unless the funding increase justifies it — a broader "under-served communities" theme is more defensible for RMIT/Leon's cross-industry sponsor outreach.
